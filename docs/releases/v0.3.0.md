**Polaris v0.3.0** redesigns the Telescope screen around two navigation modes.

## What's new
- **Camera mode**: point the back of the phone straight at the selected object. Shows where the rear camera points and an azimuth/altitude guide.
- **Phone plane mode** (default): tilt the phone until its flat plane passes through the object.
  - Spinning the phone within its own plane (top edge left or right) doesn't matter; only the plane's tilt does.
  - Using gravity, the app says which edge to raise or lower and by how much ("Raise the top-left edge 29°").
  - A live phone diagram shows the edge to tilt and where the object lies within the plane, plus a signed off-plane angle and an alignment meter.
- **Heading**: Compass (declination-corrected, with accuracy and interference warnings) or Gyro only (immune to magnetic interference, drifts slowly).
- **Star sync**: aim the back of the phone at a bright object to correct the heading for the session. It applies to both modes.
- New doc: `docs/TELESCOPE.md`.

## Changed
- The v0.2.0 "Simple" and "Mounting profile" pointing models were replaced by the two modes above. Saved mounting profiles are no longer used.

## Known limitations
- Not yet field-tested on a real phone and telescope. The math is verified by unit tests and simulated sensor input.
- Azimuth accuracy depends on the compass (2–15° bias near metal) or on gyro drift. A star sync helps.
- Plane mode confirms the object lies in the plane, not where within it.

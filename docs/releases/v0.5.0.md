**Polaris v0.5.0** adds telescope sync for pinpointing small planets, plus live tracking of moving objects.

## What's new
- **Telescope sync (Phone plane mode)**: track the Moon (or any bright object) until it's centred in the telescope, or in the phone camera looking through it, then tap **Sync**.
  - From then on, guidance follows the **telescope's measured line of sight** instead of the phone plane's vertical line. This removes the mounting offset and compass error that make small targets like Saturn hard to find.
  - Instructions like "Turn left 3.2°, raise the telescope 1.1°", with a pinpoint error and a target view.
  - **Add a second sync** on another object at a different altitude (a planet or bright star) to separate compass error from mounting offset and get accuracy across the whole sky. Per-sync residuals and fit RMS are shown.
  - Syncs are **saved** (compass mode) and reused next session while the phone stays in the same place on the telescope. A sync that disagrees by more than 5° automatically replaces the old ones (phone moved). There's a **Reset** button too.
  - Sync uses 1.5 s of averaged sensor data, is rejected if the telescope moved, and uses the object's position at the exact moment of capture.
- **Live tracking**: object positions on the Telescope screen update **every second**, so guidance follows the target as it drifts. The target card shows the live motion rate (e.g. "moving 0.24°/min, az +0.11, alt −0.22").
- Planets are now offered as sync objects.

## Known limitations
- Not yet field-tested on a real telescope. The calibration math is verified by unit tests: one sync is exact at the sync object and within 1.5° nearby; two syncs reduce a 5° compass error to under 0.1° across the sky in simulation.
- With a single sync, accuracy degrades with distance from the sync object (compass distortion varies across the sky). Add a second sync.
- Re-sync (or Reset) whenever the phone is moved on the telescope.
